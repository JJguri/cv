**Carbon Removal and MRV**

Carbon dioxide removal (CDR), enhanced rock weathering (ERW), biochar and agricultural soil carbon. Measurement, reporting and verification (MRV), digital MRV, carbon quantification and verification readiness.

**Carbon Project Development and Technical Due Diligence**

Project design documents (PDDs), monitoring plans, baseline assessment, additionality evidence and certification support. Scientific and technical due diligence for project developers, investors and carbon buyers.

**Agricultural Systems and Environmental Modelling**

Crop and soil modelling, biogeochemical modelling, regenerative agriculture, climate adaptation, crop productivity and nutrient cycling. Practical experience with APSIM, DNDC and APSIM–MIN3P modelling.

**Data Science and Geospatial Analysis**

Python, R, SQL, remote sensing, GIS and reproducible data pipelines. Integration of satellite imagery, climate records, soil measurements and farm-management data into digital agriculture and decision-support tools.

**Experimental Design and Uncertainty**

Field trials, treatment and control comparisons, soil sampling, model calibration and validation, sensitivity analysis and uncertainty quantification. Quality assurance and quality control across field, laboratory and modelling workflows.

**Leadership and Commercial Development**

Scientific leadership, multidisciplinary team building, programme management and strategic partnerships. Research funding proposals, project financial modelling, investor engagement and collaboration with farmers, industry and government.
